Questions we get a lot

Straight answers.

If it isn't here, message us on TikTok or email hello@foxmediaagency.co.uk.

Do I have to pay anything?

No. Joining Fox Media is free. We're a TikTok LIVE Creator Network agency, which means TikTok pays us a percentage. It never comes out of your gifts or diamonds. Our paid training courses and coaching are optional extras, and you'll never be pushed towards them.

How do I join?

Apply using the form on this site. We review every application, then if you're a fit we'll book an onboarding call, get your account set up and welcome you into the Discord.

Who can join, and who can't?

You need to be 18 or over, UK-based, using your main TikTok account, with the account in good standing, and willing to go LIVE regularly. We can't take under 18s, creators outside the UK, accounts with repeated violations, or anyone looking for shortcuts or fake growth.

Do I need a big following?

No. We welcome creators at any stage, whether you're just starting out or already building momentum. You don't need polished content either. You just need the drive to grow.

How do I grow on LIVE?

Consistency, confidence and knowing how to hold a room. That's exactly what we train. You'll get LIVE training, camera confidence coaching and a manager who watches your streams and tells you what to change.

How often should I go LIVE?

Regularly enough to build a routine your viewers can count on. We'll work out a schedule that fits your life. We never push unhealthy hours.

How do gifts and diamonds work?

Viewers send gifts during your LIVE. Those gifts convert to diamonds on your account, which you can cash out through TikTok. We'll walk you through exactly how it works, what the thresholds are and how to make the most of it, in plain English, during onboarding.

Do you help with content ideas?

Yes. Content strategy sessions, a Discord full of creators to bounce ideas off, and a manager you can message any day.

What support do I actually get?

An onboarding call, LIVE training, camera confidence coaching, content strategy sessions, a dedicated creator manager with daily message access and weekly check-ins, monthly group sessions, the Discord community, and access to campaigns, events, battles and collabs.

Can I leave if it's not for me?

Yes. We'd rather you were somewhere that suits you. We'll explain how leaving a Creator Network works on TikTok's side during onboarding so there are no surprises.

Where are you based?

Cornwall, with creators across the UK. Most support is online, with occasional in-person sessions for those who can get to them.
